Plan your booking

The booking hub provides the availability calendar and reservation request process for the CLAMS system.

  • Rate: $300 CAD per week for the complete system.
  • Booking period: One-week minimum, with whole-week bookings.
  • Confirmation: Reservations are confirmed by an administrator.
  • Time zone: All dates in the booking hub use Newfoundland time.

Visit the booking hub for current availability and booking details.
